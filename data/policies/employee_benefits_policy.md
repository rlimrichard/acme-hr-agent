## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy describes the employee benefits program offered by Acme Corp. Benefits are a core component of total compensation and are designed to support the health, financial security, and wellbeing of employees and their eligible dependents.

This policy applies to all regular full-time and part-time employees working **20 or more hours per week**. Contractors, consultants, and temporary workers are not eligible for benefits under this policy unless explicitly stated in their agreements.

Benefits are subject to change annually. Employees will be notified of material changes during the Open Enrollment period each November.

---

## 2. Benefits Eligibility

### 2.1 Eligibility Start Date

Benefits eligibility begins on the **first day of the month following the employee's hire date**, with one exception: if an employee is hired on the first day of a month, coverage begins on that date.

**Example:** An employee hired on March 15th becomes benefits-eligible on April 1st.

### 2.2 Dependent Eligibility

Eligible dependents include:
- Legal spouse or domestic partner (registered or unregistered with affidavit)
- Biological, adopted, or step-children up to age **26** (regardless of student or marital status)
- Children of any age who are incapable of self-support due to a qualifying disability

Employees must provide supporting documentation (marriage certificate, birth certificate, domestic partner affidavit) when enrolling dependents.

### 2.3 Part-Time Employee Benefits

Employees working 20–29 hours per week are eligible for a subset of benefits:
- Medical insurance (employee + dependents at prorated premium share)
- Dental and vision insurance
- 401(k) with employer match
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

Benefits available only to full-time employees (30+ hours/week) include: FSA, employer-paid life insurance, and short/long-term disability.

## 3. Health Insurance

### 3.1 Medical Plans

Acme Corp offers three medical plan options through Aetna. Employees may choose the plan that best fits their needs during enrollment.

| Plan             | Type | Deductible (Individual) | Out-of-Pocket Max | Employer Premium Share |
|------------------|------|-------------------------|-------------------|------------------------|
| PPO Premium      | PPO  | $500                    | $3,000            | 90%                    |
| PPO Standard     | PPO  | $1,500                  | $5,000            | 80%                    |
| HDHP + HSA       | HDHP | $2,800                  | $7,050            | 75%                    |

Premium contributions are deducted pre-tax from each paycheck. Current premium schedules are published in Workday Benefits and updated each January.

### 3.2 Dental Insurance

Acme Corp offers dental coverage through MetLife:
- **Coverage:** Preventive (100%), Basic restorative (80%), Major restorative (50%), Orthodontia (50% up to $2,000 lifetime)
- **Annual maximum benefit:** $2,000 per covered individual
- **Employer premium share:** 80% for employee; 60% for dependents

### 3.3 Vision Insurance

Vision coverage is provided through VSP:
- Eye exam: Covered in full (once per calendar year)
- Frames: $200 allowance per year
- Contact lenses: $200 allowance per year (in lieu of frames)
- Laser vision correction discount: 15% off contracted rates

Employer premium share: 100% for employee; 50% for dependents.

### 3.4 Continuation of Coverage (COBRA)

Upon separation, employees may continue medical, dental, and vision coverage under COBRA for up to 18 months (or 36 months in qualifying circumstances) at their own expense plus a 2% administrative fee. COBRA election information is mailed within 14 days of separation.

## 4. Retirement Benefits

### 4.1 401(k) Plan Overview

Acme Corp offers a 401(k) plan administered by Fidelity. All eligible employees may participate beginning on their **first day of employment**.

### 4.2 Employer Match

Acme Corp matches **100% of employee contributions up to 4% of eligible compensation**. The employer match is made per payroll period and is immediately vested.

**Example:** An employee earning $100,000 who contributes 6% ($6,000/year) receives an employer match of $4,000 (4% × $100,000).

### 4.3 Contribution Limits

Employee contributions are subject to IRS annual limits:
- **Under age 50:** $23,500 (2025 limit)
- **Age 50 and over:** $31,000 (2025 limit, including $7,500 catch-up contribution)

### 4.4 Investment Options

Participants may choose from a curated menu of Fidelity index funds and target-date funds. Financial wellness resources, including access to a Fidelity advisor, are available at no cost.

### 4.5 Vesting Schedule

Employee contributions are always 100% vested immediately. Employer contributions vest on the following schedule:

| Years of Service | Vested Percentage |
|------------------|-------------------|
| Less than 1 year | 0%                |
| 1 year           | 25%               |
| 2 years          | 50%               |
| 3 years          | 75%               |
| 4 years          | 100%              |

## 5. Life and Disability Insurance

### 5.1 Employer-Paid Life Insurance

Acme Corp provides **Basic Life Insurance** at no cost to full-time employees:
- Coverage amount: **2x annual base salary** (up to a maximum of $500,000)
- Coverage includes Accidental Death & Dismemberment (AD&D) at an equal amount
- Provided through Lincoln Financial Group

### 5.2 Supplemental Life Insurance

Employees may purchase additional life insurance for themselves and eligible dependents during Open Enrollment or within 31 days of a qualifying life event:
- **Employee supplemental:** Up to 5x salary (max $1,000,000) subject to Evidence of Insurability above guaranteed issue limits
- **Spouse/domestic partner:** Up to $250,000
- **Children:** $10,000 flat amount

### 5.3 Short-Term Disability (STD)

Acme Corp provides employer-paid STD coverage through Lincoln Financial:
- **Benefit amount:** 60% of weekly base salary
- **Elimination period:** 7 calendar days
- **Maximum duration:** 12 weeks
- Coordinates with PTO; employees may use accrued PTO to supplement STD to reach 100% of base pay during the first 12 weeks

### 5.4 Long-Term Disability (LTD)

LTD coverage activates after STD is exhausted:
- **Benefit amount:** 60% of monthly base salary (up to $10,000/month)
- **Elimination period:** 90 days (must be continuously disabled)
- **Maximum duration:** To Social Security Normal Retirement Age
- Provided through Lincoln Financial at no cost to full-time employees

## 6. Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA)

### 6.1 Healthcare FSA

Full-time employees not enrolled in the HDHP plan may contribute to a Healthcare FSA:
- **2025 employee contribution limit:** $3,300
- Funds are available in full at the start of the plan year
- Eligible expenses: medical, dental, and vision costs not covered by insurance, per IRS Publication 502
- **Carry-over provision:** Up to $640 may be carried into the following plan year; any excess above that is forfeited

### 6.2 Dependent Care FSA

Employees may also elect a Dependent Care FSA for qualified childcare and dependent care expenses:
- **2025 contribution limit:** $5,000 per household ($2,500 if married filing separately)
- Funds are available as contributions are made
- **No carry-over:** All unused funds are forfeited at year-end (IRS rule)

## 7. Health Savings Account (HSA)

Employees enrolled in the High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) are eligible for an HSA administered through Fidelity:

| Coverage Type      | 2025 Employee Contribution Limit | Acme Corp Annual Contribution |
|--------------------|----------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Individual         | $4,300                           | $750                          |
| Family             | $8,550                           | $1,500                        |

HSA funds roll over year to year with no expiration, may be invested, and are triple tax-advantaged (pre-tax contributions, tax-free growth, tax-free qualified withdrawals).

## 8. Commuter Benefits

Acme Corp offers a pre-tax commuter benefit program through WageWorks for office-based and hybrid employees:
- **Transit:** Up to $315/month (2025 IRS limit) pre-tax for public transit, vanpools, and eligible ferry costs
- **Parking:** Up to $315/month (2025 IRS limit) pre-tax for qualified parking at or near the workplace
- Elections can be changed monthly

Fully remote employees are not eligible for commuter benefits unless they regularly travel to an Acme Corp office.

## 9. Wellness Programs

### 9.1 Gym and Fitness Subsidy

All full-time employees receive a **$600 annual wellness stipend** (disbursed as $50/month via payroll) to cover gym memberships, fitness classes, or eligible wellness-related expenses. Documentation is not required; the stipend is provided as taxable income.

### 9.2 Mental Health Support

Acme Corp provides access to **Spring Health**, a mental health benefit platform:
- Up to **8 free therapy sessions per year** with licensed therapists
- Access to psychiatry, coaching, and guided self-care programs
- Available to employees and their adult household members

### 9.3 Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

The EAP through LifeWorks provides confidential support for:
- Mental health counseling (up to 6 sessions/year at no cost)
- Legal consultation (up to 30 minutes/issue with a licensed attorney)
- Financial counseling
- Work-life resource referrals (childcare, eldercare, housing)

EAP services are completely confidential and not reported to Acme Corp.

## 10. Additional Benefits

### 10.1 Parental Leave

Parental leave benefits are governed by a separate policy (POL-LOA-008). In brief:
- **Primary caregiver:** 16 weeks paid at 100% of base salary
- **Secondary caregiver:** 6 weeks paid at 100% of base salary

Employees with at least one year of service may apply for education assistance of up to **$5,250 per calendar year** (the IRS tax-free limit) for job-related courses at accredited institutions. See the Education Assistance Request form in Workday. Approval requires manager and HR sign-off; a 2-year service commitment post-reimbursement applies.

### 10.3 Employee Discount Program

Acme Corp partners with PerkSpot to provide discounts on travel, dining, retail, electronics, and more. Access via the PerkSpot portal (perkspot.acmecorp.internal).

## 11. Benefits Enrollment

### 11.1 New Hire Enrollment

New employees must enroll in benefits within **31 days of their hire date** through Workday Benefits. Failure to enroll within this window results in enrollment in default plans (medical only, no FSA/HSA elections) until the next Open Enrollment period unless a qualifying life event occurs.

### 11.2 Open Enrollment

Open Enrollment occurs each year in **November** for coverage effective January 1st of the following year. All benefit elections, including those who wish to keep existing elections, must be confirmed during Open Enrollment. Most elections cannot be changed outside of Open Enrollment without a qualifying life event.

### 11.3 Qualifying Life Events

The following events allow benefit changes outside Open Enrollment within **31 days** of the event:
- Marriage or divorce
- Birth, adoption, or placement for adoption of a child
- Death of a covered dependent
- Change in dependent eligibility
- Spouse or domestic partner gains or loses employer-sponsored coverage
- Employee's hours change, affecting eligibility
