Expense Reimbursement Policy

Document ID: POL-EXP-001
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Last Reviewed: September 1, 2025
Owner: Finance & Accounting
Version: 4.0

1. Purpose and Scope

This policy outlines the procedures and limits for reimbursing employees for business expenses incurred on behalf of Acme Corp. It applies to all regular full-time and part-time employees and any contractors explicitly authorized in their agreements.

Acme Corp reimburses employees for reasonable, necessary, and properly documented business expenses. Employees must use good judgment and spend company funds as if they were their own.

2. General Principles

2.1 Pre-Approval Requirement

Expenses exceeding $500 (per event or per trip) require written pre-approval from the employee's direct manager and a member of the Finance team before being incurred. Exceptions are not reimbursable without documented post-hoc justification approved by the CFO.

2.2 Submission Deadline

All expense reports must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the expense being incurred. Expenses submitted after 30 days may be denied. Expenses from a prior fiscal year submitted in the new fiscal year require CFO approval.

2.3 Documentation Requirements

Every expense claim must include: - Original itemized receipt (credit card statements are not acceptable as sole documentation) - Business purpose (a brief description of the meeting, event, or activity) - Attendees (for meals and entertainment: names and titles of all attendees)

2.4 Expense Submission System

All expenses must be submitted through Expensify connected to the employee's Acme Corp account. Paper forms are not accepted unless Expensify is experiencing a system outage, in which case the Finance team must be notified.

3. Travel Expenses

3.1 Airfare

  • Employees must book economy class for all domestic flights and flights under 8 hours.
  • Business class is permitted for international flights of 8 hours or more, or with explicit VP-level pre-approval.
  • Tickets must be booked through the approved travel portal (Navan) at least 14 days in advance to qualify for full reimbursement. Last-minute bookings (under 14 days) require manager justification in the expense report.
  • Seat upgrade fees are not reimbursable unless included in a standard fare.

3.2 Ground Transportation

  • Rideshare (Uber/Lyft): Reimbursable for airport transfers and business travel. Standard tier only; premium/black tiers require manager pre-approval.
  • Taxis: Reimbursable where rideshare is unavailable.
  • Personal Vehicle: Reimbursed at the current IRS standard mileage rate (updated annually). Employees must log mileage in Expensify with start/end addresses.
  • Rental Cars: Economy or compact class only. Insurance through the corporate travel portal is required; personal insurance add-ons are not reimbursable.

3.3 Lodging

  • Hotel bookings must be made through Navan.
  • The nightly rate limit varies by city tier:
City Tier Nightly Limit (Before Tax)
Tier 1 (NYC, SF, London, Tokyo) $350
Tier 2 (Chicago, LA, Boston, Paris) $275
Tier 3 (All other cities) $200
  • Employees may choose to stay with friends/family. In that case, a $50/night goodwill gift to the host is reimbursable with a receipt.
  • Incidental charges (room service, minibar, hotel gym) are not reimbursable.

3.4 Internet and Phone

  • Business-use internet costs (e.g., hotel Wi-Fi, airport Wi-Fi) are reimbursable.
  • Employees on approved full-time remote work arrangements receive a $50/month internet stipend, processed automatically through payroll (not via expense report). See Remote Work Policy (POL-RW-001), Section 4.3.
  • International roaming charges incurred for business calls are reimbursable with a detailed call log.

3.5 Meals While Traveling

Daily meal per diems while traveling on company business (away from home office city):

Meal Per Diem Limit
Breakfast $20
Lunch $30
Dinner $75
Daily Total $125

Per diems are for individual meals. Actual receipts are required; per diem is a cap, not a flat payment.

4. Meals and Entertainment

4.1 Business Meals (Non-Travel)

Business meals with clients, candidates, or cross-functional partners are reimbursable. The limit is $100 per person including food and non-alcoholic beverages. The business purpose and attendee list must be documented.

4.2 Alcohol

Alcohol is reimbursable in the context of client entertainment or team events, subject to a limit of $30 per person. Alcohol may never be the sole item on an expense report.

4.3 Team Meals and Celebrations

Manager-approved team meals and celebrations are reimbursable up to $75 per person. Budget must be approved in writing before the event.

4.4 Client Entertainment

Client entertainment (events, sporting events, concerts) is reimbursable up to $200 per client per event with VP pre-approval. The specific client and business relationship must be documented.

5. Technology and Office Supplies

5.1 Software and SaaS Subscriptions

Personal software purchases require IT pre-approval. All SaaS tools must be vetted for security compliance. Employees should not purchase subscriptions that duplicate existing company-provided tools.

5.2 Office Supplies (Remote)

Remote employees may expense up to $30/month for consumable office supplies (paper, pens, printer ink). Receipts required. This limit does not apply to ergonomic equipment, which is governed by the Remote Work Policy (POL-RW-001, Section 5.2).

5.3 Books and Learning Materials

Books and courses directly related to job responsibilities are reimbursable up to $200/year per employee without pre-approval. Above $200, manager approval is required. Online learning platform subscriptions must be approved by the employee's manager and People Operations.

6. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following expenses are never reimbursable regardless of business context: - Personal meals while not traveling - Traffic fines, parking tickets, or moving violations - Childcare or pet care - Personal travel extensions or leisure activities during business trips - First-class airfare (unless medically required with documentation) - Gifts to employees (governed by the Gifts and Gratuities Policy, POL-HR-009) - Lost or stolen cash - Personal subscriptions unrelated to job function - Expenses submitted more than 90 days after being incurred (no exceptions)

7. Expense Report Approval Workflow

7.1 Standard Flow

  1. Employee submits report in Expensify.
  2. Direct manager approves within 5 business days.
  3. Finance reviews for policy compliance within 3 business days.
  4. Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle via direct deposit.

7.2 Escalation

If a manager fails to act on an expense report within 5 business days, the employee may escalate to Finance directly. Finance has authority to approve or deny without manager action if escalated.

7.3 Disputes

Employees who disagree with a denial may submit a written dispute to finance-disputes@acmecorp.com within 10 business days of notification. Finance will respond within 5 business days.

8. Fraud and Misuse

Intentional misrepresentation of expenses constitutes fraud and will result in disciplinary action up to and including termination, repayment of the fraudulent amount, and potential legal action. Acme Corp reserves the right to audit all expense reports at any time.
