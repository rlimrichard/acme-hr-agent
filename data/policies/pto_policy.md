**Last Reviewed:** September 1, 2025

## 1. Purpose and Scope

Acme Corp's Paid Time Off (PTO) policy provides employees with flexible, paid time away from work for vacations, personal matters, illness, and other personal needs. This policy consolidates vacation, personal, and sick days into a single unified PTO bank.

This policy applies to all regular full-time and part-time employees. Contractors, temporary workers, and interns are not eligible unless otherwise specified in their agreements.

---

## 2. PTO Accrual

### 2.1 Full-Time Employee Accrual Rates

PTO is accrued on a per-pay-period basis (every two weeks). Accrual rates are based on years of continuous service:

| Years of Service       | Annual PTO Days | Accrual per Pay Period |
|------------------------|-----------------|------------------------|
| 0 – 1 year             | 15 days         | 0.577 days             |
| 1 – 3 years            | 18 days         | 0.692 days             |
| 3 – 7 years            | 22 days         | 0.846 days             |
| 7+ years               | 25 days         | 0.962 days             |

### 2.2 Part-Time Employee Accrual

Part-time employees accrue PTO on a prorated basis relative to a standard 40-hour work week. For example, an employee working 20 hours per week accrues PTO at 50% of the full-time rate for their tenure tier.

### 2.3 PTO Accrual Start Date

PTO begins accruing from the employee's first day of employment. However, PTO may not be used until the employee has completed their **30-day introductory period**, except in documented medical emergencies at manager and HR discretion.

## 3. PTO Usage

### 3.1 Requesting Time Off

All PTO requests must be submitted through the **Workday HR Portal** with a minimum advance notice of:
- **Vacation (3+ consecutive days):** 10 business days' notice
- **Personal days (1–2 days):** 3 business days' notice
- **Unplanned sick leave:** Notify your manager and team as soon as reasonably possible, ideally before the start of the business day

### 3.2 Approval Process

PTO requests are approved by the employee's direct manager. Managers must respond to PTO requests within **3 business days**. Requests may be denied if they conflict with critical business needs, project deadlines, or if minimum team coverage cannot be maintained.

### 3.3 Minimum Increment

PTO is taken in minimum increments of **4 hours (half day)**. Shorter absences are handled at manager discretion and may not require formal PTO submission.

### 3.4 Blackout Periods

Certain high-demand periods may be designated as PTO blackout periods by department heads with advance notice of at least 60 days. During blackout periods, PTO requests may only be approved for pre-approved exceptions (e.g., pre-booked travel, medical necessity).

---

## 4. PTO Carryover

### 4.1 Annual Carryover Cap

Unused PTO may be carried over into the following calendar year, subject to a maximum carryover cap:

| Years of Service | Carryover Cap  |
|------------------|----------------|
| 0 – 3 years      | 5 days         |
| 3 – 7 years      | 8 days         |
| 7+ years         | 10 days        |

Any PTO balance exceeding the carryover cap as of **December 31st** will be forfeited. Employees will receive automated reminders from Workday starting in October.

### 4.2 Exceptions

Employees on approved leave of absence (medical, parental, or military) during the year-end period may apply for a one-time carryover exception through People Operations.

---

## 5. PTO Payout Upon Separation

### 5.1 Voluntary Resignation

Employees who resign with at least **10 business days' written notice** will receive a payout of their accrued, unused PTO balance at their current base rate of pay, subject to applicable state law.

### 5.2 Involuntary Termination

In cases of termination without cause, accrued, unused PTO will be paid out in the final paycheck per applicable state law.

### 5.3 Termination for Cause

Accrued PTO payout in cases of termination for cause is governed by applicable state law. In jurisdictions where payout is not legally required, Acme Corp will not pay out accrued PTO upon termination for cause.

10. Christmas Eve
11. Christmas Day
12. New Year's Eve

### 6.2 Floating Holidays

In addition to the above, each employee receives **2 floating holidays** per calendar year, which may be used on any day of their choosing with standard PTO request notice. Floating holidays do not carry over and have no cash value upon separation.

### 6.3 Holidays Falling on Weekends

If a company holiday falls on a Saturday, it is observed on the preceding Friday. If it falls on a Sunday, it is observed on the following Monday.

## 7. Sick Leave

### 7.1 Use of PTO for Sick Leave

Acme Corp uses a unified PTO bank; there is no separate sick leave bucket. Employees use their PTO balance for illness, medical appointments, and caring for ill dependents.

### 7.2 Extended Illness

For absences exceeding **5 consecutive business days**, employees must contact People Operations to initiate a Short-Term Disability (STD) claim. STD benefits kick in after a 7-calendar-day waiting period and provide 60% of base salary for up to 90 days.

### 7.3 Documentation

Acme Corp may request a physician's note for absences of 3 or more consecutive days. The note need not disclose a diagnosis—confirmation that the employee was unable to work is sufficient.

---

## 8. Bereavement Leave

### 8.1 Bereavement Entitlement

Bereavement leave is provided separately from PTO and does not draw from an employee's PTO balance:

| Relationship                         | Paid Leave |
|--------------------------------------|------------|
| Spouse, domestic partner, child      | 5 days     |
| Parent, sibling, grandparent         | 3 days     |
| In-law, grandchild, close friend     | 1 day      |

### 8.2 Extended Bereavement

Additional time needed beyond the above entitlement may be covered by PTO at the employee's request and manager approval.

---

## 9. Parental Leave

Parental leave is governed by a separate policy (POL-PL-003) and does not draw from PTO balances. Employees should refer to that policy or contact People Operations for details.
