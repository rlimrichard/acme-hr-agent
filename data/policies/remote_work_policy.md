# Remote Work Policy

**Document ID:** POL-RW-001  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** September 1, 2025  
**Owner:** People Operations  
**Version:** 2.3

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Remote Work Policy establishes guidelines and expectations for employees of Acme Corp who work remotely, whether on a full-time, hybrid, or occasional basis. It applies to all regular full-time and part-time employees in eligible roles.

This policy does not apply to contractors, freelancers, or temporary staff unless explicitly stated in their contracts.

---

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Role-Based Eligibility

Remote work eligibility is determined at the role level by the employee's department head in collaboration with People Operations. Roles that require physical presence (e.g., facilities, on-site lab work, reception) are not eligible.

Eligible roles are classified as:
- **Fully Remote:** Employee may work remotely 100% of the time.
- **Hybrid:** Employee is expected to be on-site a minimum number of days per week or month as defined by their team agreement.
- **Office-First with Flex Days:** Employee's primary work location is an Acme Corp office but may work remotely up to 2 days per week at manager discretion.

### 2.2 Employee Standing Requirements

To be eligible for remote work arrangements, an employee must:
- Have completed their 90-day onboarding period.
- Be in good standing with no active Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs).
- Have received a satisfactory or above rating in their most recent performance review.

---

## 3. Work Hours and Availability

### 3.1 Core Hours

All remote employees must be reachable and responsive during **core hours of 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM in their designated time zone**, unless otherwise agreed upon with their manager in writing.

### 3.2 Schedule Communication

Remote employees must maintain an up-to-date calendar and mark any blocks where they are unavailable. Extended absences of more than two hours during core hours must be communicated to the team in advance.

### 3.3 Overtime and After-Hours Work

Working outside of normal business hours does not automatically qualify for overtime compensation. Employees covered by the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) who are non-exempt must obtain written pre-approval from their manager before working overtime.

---

## 4. Equipment and Technology

### 4.1 Company-Provided Equipment

Acme Corp will provide the following standard equipment to remote employees:
- One (1) laptop (MacBook Pro or equivalent Windows device, per IT approval)
- One (1) external monitor (27" standard)
- Standard peripherals: keyboard, mouse, headset

Requests for additional equipment must be submitted via the IT Service Portal and are subject to budget approval.

### 4.2 Personal Equipment

Personal devices may not be used to access Acme Corp systems unless enrolled in the Mobile Device Management (MDM) program. Employees using personal equipment do so at their own risk and must comply with all security requirements outlined in Section 6.

### 4.3 Internet Connectivity

Employees are responsible for maintaining a reliable internet connection with a minimum download speed of **25 Mbps**. Acme Corp does not reimburse internet service costs unless the employee is in a designated stipend-eligible role (see Expense Reimbursement Policy POL-EXP-001, Section 3.4).

---

## 5. Home Office Requirements

### 5.1 Dedicated Workspace

Remote employees are strongly encouraged to maintain a dedicated, quiet workspace that minimizes interruptions during working hours. This workspace must allow for private conversations when handling confidential business matters.

### 5.2 Ergonomics

Acme Corp provides a one-time **ergonomics stipend of $500** for qualifying remote employees to set up their home office. Receipts must be submitted within 90 days of the stipend approval date. Eligible purchases include:
- Ergonomic chairs
- Standing desks or desk converters
- Monitor arms and risers
- Footrests and wrist supports

The stipend is not available for general office supplies, décor, or non-ergonomic furniture.

---

## 6. Security and Data Privacy

### 6.1 VPN Usage

All remote employees must connect to Acme Corp systems through the company-approved VPN at all times. Accessing internal tools, documents, or customer data without an active VPN connection is a policy violation.

### 6.2 Data Handling

Remote employees must adhere to Acme Corp's Data Classification Policy (POL-SEC-004). Confidential or restricted data must never be stored on personal devices or unencrypted external drives.

### 6.3 Physical Security

Remote employees are responsible for ensuring their workspace is physically secure. Screens must be locked when unattended. Confidential documents must not be left visible to household members or guests.

### 6.4 Incident Reporting

Any security incident—including lost or stolen equipment, unauthorized access, or suspected phishing—must be reported to the IT Security team within **one (1) hour** of discovery.

---

## 7. Performance and Communication

### 7.1 Expectations

Remote employees are held to the same performance standards as on-site employees. Managers are responsible for setting clear, measurable goals and conducting regular check-ins (at minimum bi-weekly).

### 7.2 Communication Tools

Acme Corp uses the following tools for remote communication. All remote employees must have active accounts and remain proficient in:
- **Slack:** Primary async messaging
- **Zoom:** Video meetings and standups
- **Confluence:** Documentation and knowledge sharing
- **Jira:** Project and task tracking

### 7.3 Response Time Expectations

During core hours, employees should respond to Slack messages within **30 minutes** and to emails within **4 hours**. These are guidelines, not strict SLAs, and context may vary.

---

## 8. In-Office and Travel Requirements

### 8.1 Mandatory On-Site Events

All employees—regardless of remote status—are required to attend the following in-person events unless granted a formal exemption:
- Quarterly All-Hands meetings
- Annual team offsite
- New hire onboarding week (for employees within commuting distance)

### 8.2 Travel Expenses

Travel to Acme Corp offices for mandatory events is fully reimbursed per the Expense Reimbursement Policy (POL-EXP-001). Employees should book travel at least 14 days in advance through the approved travel portal to ensure cost-effective fares.

---

## 9. Policy Violations

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including revocation of remote work privileges or termination of employment, depending on severity. Violations include but are not limited to:
- Repeated unresponsiveness during core hours without notification
- Accessing company systems without VPN
- Storing confidential data on unapproved personal devices
- Failure to report security incidents in a timely manner
