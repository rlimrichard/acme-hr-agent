# Onboarding Policy

**Document ID:** POL-ONB-006  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** September 1, 2025  
**Owner:** People Operations  
**Version:** 2.1

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy defines the structured onboarding process for all new employees joining Acme Corp. A successful onboarding experience is critical for employee productivity, engagement, and long-term retention. This policy applies to all regular full-time and part-time employees.

Managers and hiring teams share responsibility for executing onboarding steps within their respective domains. People Operations coordinates and oversees the overall onboarding program.

---

## 2. Pre-Arrival (Before Day 1)

### 2.1 People Operations Responsibilities

At least **5 business days before the start date**, People Operations will:
- Send the new hire an offer confirmation email with start date, time, reporting location (or virtual meeting link), and dress code guidance.
- Initiate the background check process if not yet complete; start date is contingent on a satisfactory background check result.
- Send the new hire's Welcome Kit, which includes links to complete I-9 documentation, direct deposit setup, benefits enrollment overview, and IT onboarding forms.
- Create the employee's record in Workday and assign mandatory training courses.

### 2.2 Hiring Manager Responsibilities

At least **3 business days before the start date**, the hiring manager must:
- Submit the system access request via the IT Service Portal, specifying required tools, permissions, and application access.
- Assign an **Onboarding Buddy** from the team (a peer, not the direct manager) who will serve as an informal guide for the new hire's first 90 days.
- Prepare a written **30/60/90-Day Plan** to share with the new hire on Day 1.
- Ensure a workspace is ready: physical desk assignment (if office-based) or equipment shipped in time (if remote — see POL-EQP-007, Section 2.1).
- Schedule recurring 1:1 check-ins for the first 90 days (minimum: weekly for the first 30 days, bi-weekly for days 31–90).

### 2.3 IT Responsibilities

Upon receiving the access request, IT will:
- Provision the new hire's corporate email account and send login credentials to their personal email address.
- Configure device enrollment and ship equipment for remote employees at least **3 business days before start date**.
- Enable access to standard tool suite: Slack, Zoom, Confluence, and Jira.

## 3. Day 1

### 3.1 Welcome and Orientation

New hires will attend a virtual or in-person **New Hire Orientation** session facilitated by People Operations. Day 1 orientation covers:
- Company mission, values, and culture
- Org structure and key leadership introductions
- HR systems walkthrough (Workday, benefits enrollment, PTO requests)
- Payroll schedule and direct deposit confirmation
- Security awareness briefing and acceptable use acknowledgment
- Office/virtual tour and introduction to key resources

### 3.2 Manager Introduction

The hiring manager meets with the new hire on Day 1 to:
- Review the team's mission, current priorities, and the new hire's role within it.
- Share the 30/60/90-Day Plan.
- Introduce the new hire to immediate team members.
- Set expectations for the first week.

### 3.3 Buddy Introduction

The Onboarding Buddy connects with the new hire on Day 1 via a scheduled introduction meeting. The buddy's role is informal mentorship and answering day-to-day questions outside of formal channels.

### 3.4 Required Day 1 Actions by New Hire

The new hire must complete the following on their first day:
- Sign and acknowledge receipt of the Employee Handbook.
- Complete I-9 employment verification documentation (in person or via authorized remote notary).
- Set up MFA on all company accounts (see POL-SEC-004, Section 4.2).
- Confirm direct deposit details in Workday.
- Enroll in benefits (31-day window begins today; see POL-BEN-005, Section 11.1).

---

## 4. First Week (Days 1–5)

### 4.1 Required Training Completions

All new hires must complete the following mandatory training within their **first 7 calendar days**:
- Security Awareness Training (assigned in Workday Learning)
- Workplace Conduct and Anti-Harassment Training
- Data Classification and Handling Training
- Privacy and GDPR Awareness (required for all roles with access to customer data)

Failure to complete within 7 days will result in escalation to the employee's manager.

### 4.2 Tool and System Familiarization

During the first week, IT and the Onboarding Buddy will walk the new hire through:
- Internal documentation and wiki (Confluence)
- Task and project management (Jira)
- Code repositories (GitHub) for technical roles
- Internal communication norms and Slack channel directory
- Expense reporting (Expensify) per POL-EXP-001

### 4.3 Key Meetings

New hires should attend:
- Team standup or weekly sync
- Any scheduled all-hands or department meeting during the first week
- Skip-level introduction with the skip-level manager (to be scheduled by manager within first 2 weeks)

## 5. 30-Day Milestone

### 5.1 Manager Check-In

At the 30-day mark, the manager conducts a structured check-in covering:
- Progress against the 30-day goals in the 30/60/90-Day Plan
- Role clarity: does the new hire understand their responsibilities and success metrics?
- Blockers, resource needs, and feedback on the onboarding experience
- Adjustment of goals for the next 30 days if needed

### 5.2 People Operations Survey

People Operations will send the new hire a short **30-Day Onboarding Survey** to gather feedback on the onboarding experience. Responses are confidential and used to improve the program.

### 5.3 Benefits Enrollment Deadline

If not already completed, benefits enrollment must be finalized by the end of the 31-day window. New hires who miss this window must wait until Open Enrollment in November unless a qualifying life event occurs.

---

## 6. 60-Day Milestone

### 6.1 Progress and Development Review

At 60 days, the manager and new hire review:
- Achievement of 30–60 day goals
- Skill gaps identified and development resources to address them
- Integration with the team: collaboration quality, relationships, and cultural fit
- Any concerns or performance questions surfaced early

### 6.2 Access and Permissions Review

IT and the manager review the new hire's system access to confirm it is appropriate for their role—neither over-provisioned nor missing necessary permissions—and make adjustments as needed.

---

## 7. 90-Day Milestone (End of Introductory Period)

### 7.1 Introductory Period Completion

The first 90 days constitute the **Introductory Period**. This is an evaluative period for both the employee and Acme Corp to confirm mutual fit. At 90 days:
- The manager completes a formal **90-Day Performance Check-In** via Workday.
- The check-in is documented and kept in the employee's personnel file.
- It is not a formal performance review but sets the baseline for the first full review cycle.

### 7.2 PTO and Remote Work Eligibility

Upon successful completion of the introductory period:
- Employees become eligible to **use** accrued PTO (PTO has been accruing since Day 1; see POL-PTO-002, Section 2.3).
- Employees may apply for hybrid or remote work arrangements per the Remote Work Policy (POL-RW-001, Section 2.2).

### 7.3 Onboarding Buddy Program Conclusion

The formal Onboarding Buddy relationship concludes at 90 days. Employees and buddies are encouraged to maintain the relationship informally.

## 8. Role-Specific Onboarding Tracks

### 8.1 Engineering Track

In addition to general onboarding, engineers complete:
- Repository access and Git workflow orientation
- Code review standards and contribution guide review
- Architecture overview session with the tech lead
- Shadow a sprint cycle before taking independent work

### 8.2 People Manager Track

New managers joining Acme Corp complete:
- Manager Foundations training (4-hour course in Workday Learning) within 30 days
- Briefing with HR Business Partner on performance management processes
- Review of all direct reports' current performance and development status

### 8.3 Customer-Facing Roles

Employees in Sales, Support, and Customer Success complete:
- Product certification training within first 30 days
- Shadowing of customer calls/meetings in weeks 2–4
- Introduction to CRM (Salesforce) and ticketing systems

---

## 9. Rehires and Internal Transfers

### 9.1 Rehires

Employees returning to Acme Corp within 12 months of departure may have prior accrued PTO balances and tenure recognized at HR's discretion. Rehires must complete abbreviated onboarding (Day 1 orientation, mandatory training, and system access re-provisioning).

### 9.2 Internal Transfers

Employees transferring between teams or roles complete a tailored onboarding with their new manager. A new 30/60/90-Day Plan is required. PTO balances, tenure, and benefits elections carry over without interruption.
