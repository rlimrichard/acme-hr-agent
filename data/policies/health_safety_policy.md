## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes Acme Corp's commitment to maintaining safe, healthy working environments and sets out the obligations of the company and its employees under applicable occupational health and safety laws, including OSHA (Occupational Safety and Health Act).

This policy applies to all Acme Corp offices, labs, and common areas, and to all employees, contractors, and visitors in those locations.

> **Scope boundaries:**
> - Home office ergonomics **stipend amounts and eligibility** are in POL-RW-001 §5.2. This policy covers ergonomics standards for **in-office** employees and injury prevention principles.
> - Mental health **benefits resources** (EAP, Spring Health) are in POL-BEN-005 §9. This policy addresses the employer's duty to support a psychologically safe workplace as a safety matter.
> - General workplace **conduct standards** are in POL-WPC-009. This policy addresses the physical safety dimension of conduct (violence prevention, substance-related safety).

## 2. Roles and Responsibilities

### 2.1 Company Responsibilities

Acme Corp is committed to:
- Maintaining all offices in compliance with OSHA standards and applicable local building and fire codes
- Conducting regular safety inspections of all facilities (quarterly)
- Providing safety training, first aid resources, and emergency procedures
- Investigating all workplace injuries and illnesses promptly
- Maintaining an OSHA 300 injury and illness log and posting the annual summary as required

### 2.2 Employee Responsibilities

All employees are expected to:
- Follow all safety policies, procedures, and signage
- Use equipment, ergonomic tools, and safety devices as instructed
- Report unsafe conditions, near-misses, and injuries immediately
- Participate in safety training and emergency drills
- Not interfere with safety equipment or emergency systems

### 2.3 Safety Committee

Acme Corp maintains a **Workplace Safety Committee** composed of representatives from Facilities, People Operations, IT, and at least two non-management employee volunteers. The committee meets monthly and is responsible for:
- Reviewing incident reports and near-misses
- Conducting quarterly walk-through inspections
- Recommending and tracking corrective actions
- Reviewing and updating emergency procedures annually

## 3. In-Office Ergonomics

### 3.1 Workstation Setup

Acme Corp provides ergonomic workstation assessments for all in-office employees upon request and for any employee experiencing discomfort. Employees should contact Facilities to schedule an assessment.

Standard ergonomic principles for in-office workstations:
- **Monitor height:** Top of the screen at or slightly below eye level
- **Keyboard and mouse:** Elbows at approximately 90°, wrists neutral
- **Chair:** Seat height adjusted so feet rest flat on the floor; lumbar support engaged
- **Monitor distance:** Approximately arm's length (50–70 cm) from eyes

### 3.2 Ergonomic Equipment

In-office employees may request ergonomic equipment adjustments (alternative chair, keyboard tray, monitor arm, sit-stand desk conversion) through Facilities. Standard requests are approved within **5 business days**. Requests based on a documented medical need are prioritized and coordinated with People Operations under the ADA accommodation process (POL-DEI-014 §6).

### 3.3 Repetitive Strain Prevention

Employees performing intensive keyboard or mouse work are encouraged to:
- Take a 5-minute break every 60 minutes of continuous screen work
- Use keyboard shortcuts to reduce mouse reliance
- Vary tasks throughout the day where feasible

## 4. Workplace Injury and Illness Reporting

### 4.1 Reporting Requirement

**All work-related injuries, illnesses, and near-misses must be reported**, regardless of severity, as follows:
- **Immediate life-threatening emergencies:** Call 911, then notify your manager and Facilities
- **Non-emergency injuries or illnesses:** Notify your manager and Facilities within **24 hours** of the incident
- **Near-misses (no injury but unsafe condition observed):** Report via the Safety Reporting Form on the Facilities Portal within **48 hours**

Failure to report a work-related injury may affect Workers' Compensation eligibility and constitutes a policy violation.

### 4.2 Workers' Compensation

Acme Corp provides Workers' Compensation insurance covering employees for work-related injuries and illnesses. Workers' Compensation provides:
- Coverage of medical treatment costs for the work-related condition
- Wage replacement (typically 66.67% of average weekly wage) during periods of disability
- Return-to-work and vocational rehabilitation support where applicable

To initiate a Workers' Compensation claim, notify People Operations at people-ops@acmecorp.com or call the dedicated Workers' Comp line: +1-800-555-SAFE. Claims must be filed within the timeframe specified by the employee's state law (typically 30 days from the date of injury).

### 4.3 Incident Investigation

All workplace injuries and significant near-misses are investigated by the Safety Committee within **5 business days** to identify root causes and prevent recurrence. Investigations are fact-finding, not fault-finding. Employee cooperation is required.

## 5. Emergency Procedures

### 5.1 Evacuation Procedures

Each Acme Corp office maintains a posted **Emergency Evacuation Plan** identifying:
- Designated evacuation routes and exits
- Assembly points outside the building
- Roles of designated floor wardens

Employees must familiarize themselves with the evacuation plan for their floor within their first week at a new office location. Evacuation drills are conducted **twice per year** at each office.

### 5.2 Fire Safety

- Fire extinguishers are located at marked positions throughout each office and inspected monthly by Facilities.
- Emergency exit doors must never be propped open or obstructed.
- Employees must not use elevators during fire evacuations.
- In the event of a fire alarm: evacuate immediately; do not gather belongings; do not re-enter until cleared by emergency services.

### 5.3 Medical Emergencies

- AED (automated external defibrillator) devices are located at marked positions in all offices with more than 50 employees.
- First aid kits are stocked and inspected monthly by Facilities.
- Employees certified in CPR/first aid are encouraged to register with Facilities so they can be located quickly in an emergency.
- For a medical emergency: call 911 first, then notify the office manager or Facilities team.

### 5.4 Active Threat and Security Incidents

In the event of a security threat or active threat situation:
- Follow the **Run, Hide, Fight** protocol posted in each office.
- Call 911 immediately if safe to do so.
- Notify your manager and Facilities as soon as you are in a safe location.
- Do not return to the building until cleared by law enforcement.

All employees are required to complete **active threat awareness training** (available in Workday Learning) within 30 days of joining and every 2 years thereafter.

## 6. Substance-Related Safety

### 6.1 Safety Obligation

The substance use standards in POL-WPC-009 §8 address employee conduct. This section addresses the **workplace safety obligation**: employees who observe a colleague appearing impaired while operating equipment, driving on company business, or performing safety-sensitive duties must report it to their manager or People Operations immediately. This is a safety obligation, not an optional courtesy.

### 6.2 Drug and Alcohol Testing

Acme Corp conducts drug and alcohol testing in the following circumstances, in compliance with applicable state law:
- **Post-incident:** Following any workplace accident or near-miss that could have resulted in injury
- **Reasonable suspicion:** When a manager and HR, in consultation, have objective reason to believe impairment is present
- **Pre-employment:** For roles designated as safety-sensitive (defined by the relevant department head with HR approval)

Testing is conducted by a third-party occupational health provider. Refusal to submit to a required test is treated as a positive result.

## 7. Psychological Safety and Wellbeing

### 7.1 Acme Corp's Obligation

Beyond physical safety, Acme Corp recognizes the duty to protect employees from psychologically unsafe working conditions — including chronic overwork, hostile management, bullying, and unreasonable pressure.

### 7.2 Workload and Rest

Managers must not routinely require employees to work hours or at an intensity that compromises health. Employees are encouraged to take their entitled PTO (POL-PTO-002) and are not expected to be reachable outside of core hours (POL-RW-001 §3.1) absent exceptional circumstances.

Employees who feel their workload is unsustainable should raise it first with their manager. If unresolved, they may escalate to their HR Business Partner without fear of retaliation.

### 7.3 Access to Mental Health Support

Employees experiencing mental health challenges related to work or personal circumstances are encouraged to use the support resources described in POL-BEN-005 §9 (Spring Health, EAP). Requesting mental health support will never negatively affect an employee's performance record.

## 8. Compliance and Inspections

### 8.1 OSHA Compliance

Acme Corp maintains all required OSHA postings (Form 300A annual summary) in prominent locations in each office. Employees have the right to review the OSHA 300 log upon request and to file a complaint with OSHA without retaliation.

### 8.2 External Inspections

In the event of an OSHA or regulatory safety inspection, employees must cooperate fully and honestly. Notify Facilities and Legal immediately upon being contacted by or becoming aware of an impending regulatory inspection.
