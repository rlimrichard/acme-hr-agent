1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes the schedule of paid company holidays and related entitlements for Acme Corp employees. It applies to all regular full-time and part-time employees in the United States. Employees based outside the United States should refer to their local HR addendum for jurisdiction-specific holiday schedules.

This policy should be read in conjunction with the Paid Time Off Policy (POL-PTO-002), which governs floating holidays, PTO accrual, and sick leave.

2. Observed Company Holidays

2.2 Holidays Falling on Weekends

When an observed holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, it is substituted as follows: - Saturday holiday: Observed on the preceding Friday. - Sunday holiday: Observed on the following Monday.

Employees who normally work weekends should consult their manager and People Operations for alternate day observance.

2.3 Holiday Calendar Updates

Acme Corp reserves the right to add, remove, or reschedule observed holidays with a minimum of 60 days' advance notice to employees. Changes driven by regulatory requirements may occur with shorter notice; affected employees will be notified promptly.

3. Floating Holidays

3.1 Entitlement

Each employee receives two (2) floating holidays per calendar year in addition to the observed holidays listed in Section 2.1. Floating holidays are intended to provide flexibility for observing religious, cultural, or personal occasions not covered by the standard holiday schedule.

3.2 Usage Rules

  • Floating holidays must be requested and approved through Workday following standard PTO request procedures (see POL-PTO-002, Section 3.1).
  • Minimum advance notice: 3 business days.
  • Floating holidays may be taken as full days or half days (minimum 4 hours).
  • They may not be split across multiple days in increments smaller than half a day.

3.3 Carryover and Cash-Out Restrictions

Floating holidays do not carry over to the following calendar year. Any unused floating holidays expire on December 31st of the year in which they were granted. Floating holidays have no cash value and will not be paid out upon separation from the company.

Employees who were hired after July 1st receive one (1) floating holiday for their first partial year.

4. Holiday Pay

4.1 Full-Time Employees

Regular full-time employees receive paid time off for each observed holiday at their standard base rate of pay. Holiday pay is included automatically in regular payroll and does not require a separate submission.

4.2 Part-Time Employees

Part-time employees receive holiday pay on a prorated basis proportional to their scheduled weekly hours relative to a 40-hour standard work week.

Example: An employee scheduled for 24 hours per week receives 60% of the standard holiday pay (24 ÷ 40 = 0.60).

4.3 Hourly Employees

Non-exempt hourly employees receive holiday pay equal to their standard scheduled hours for the day of the holiday (e.g., if normally scheduled for 8 hours, they receive 8 hours of pay at their regular rate).

4.4 Employees on Leave

Employees on an approved paid leave of absence (e.g., Short-Term Disability) will receive holiday pay if the holiday falls within their leave period, per applicable law and the terms of the leave. Employees on unpaid leave do not receive holiday pay for holidays occurring during the unpaid period.

5. Working on a Company Holiday

5.1 Non-Exempt Employees

Non-exempt employees required to work on a company holiday will receive their standard holiday pay plus their regular hourly rate for all hours worked on that day (effectively double-time). Overtime rules apply to any hours worked beyond the standard shift, consistent with applicable federal and state law.

5.2 Exempt Employees

Exempt employees required to work on a company holiday will receive an alternate holiday day to be used within 90 calendar days, subject to manager approval. Alternate days do not carry cash value and do not carry over beyond the 90-day window.

5.3 On-Call Obligations

Employees who are on-call over a holiday but are not actually called in are entitled to full holiday pay without reduction.

6. New Hires and Departing Employees

6.1 New Hires

New employees are eligible for holiday pay from their first day of employment. There is no waiting period for company holiday entitlement.

6.2 Termination Near a Holiday

An employee whose last day of employment falls before an observed company holiday is not entitled to holiday pay for that holiday unless required by applicable state law. Employees are encouraged to review their state's specific wage payment requirements.

7. International and Remote Employees

7.1 US-Based Remote Employees

Remote employees based in the United States follow the same holiday schedule as on-site employees, regardless of their home state's public holiday schedule.

7.2 Non-US Employees

Employees based outside the United States are subject to the holiday schedule of their employment country as defined in their local HR addendum. Local statutory holidays may differ from the US schedule above. Non-US employees should contact their regional HR representative for their applicable schedule.

8. Religious and Cultural Accommodations

Acme Corp is committed to reasonably accommodating employees' sincerely held religious beliefs and cultural observances. Employees who need time off for a religious or cultural holiday not covered by the standard schedule or floating holidays may:

  • Use accrued PTO (see POL-PTO-002).
  • Request an unpaid personal day with manager approval.
  • Swap a shift with a willing colleague (with manager approval).

Requests for religious accommodation should be made to People Operations at least 10 business days in advance where possible.
