## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy defines Acme Corp's leave of absence (LOA) programs, covering leaves beyond standard PTO as defined in the Paid Time Off Policy (POL-PTO-002). It establishes employee entitlements, eligibility requirements, pay continuation, benefits maintenance, and return-to-work procedures.

This policy applies to all regular full-time and part-time employees. Leave entitlements are subject to applicable federal and state law; where state law provides greater benefits than this policy, state law governs. Employees should contact People Operations for state-specific addenda.

---

## 2. Family and Medical Leave (FMLA)

### 2.1 FMLA Eligibility

Employees are eligible for federal FMLA leave if they:
- Have been employed by Acme Corp for at least **12 months**
- Have worked at least **1,250 hours** in the 12 months preceding the leave
- Work at a location with 50 or more employees within 75 miles

### 2.2 FMLA-Qualifying Reasons

FMLA provides up to **12 weeks of job-protected, unpaid leave** per rolling 12-month period for:
- The birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child (within 12 months of the event)
- A serious health condition that makes the employee unable to perform job functions
- Caring for a spouse, child, or parent with a serious health condition
- A qualifying exigency related to a family member's active military duty

Employees needing leave to care for a covered servicemember with a serious injury or illness may receive up to **26 weeks** of FMLA in a single 12-month period.

### 2.3 FMLA Pay Continuation

FMLA leave is unpaid under federal law. Acme Corp requires employees to use the following concurrently with FMLA leave, in order:
1. Accrued PTO (all available)
2. Short-Term Disability benefits, if applicable (see POL-BEN-005, Section 5.3)

Once accrued PTO and applicable disability benefits are exhausted, the remainder of FMLA leave is unpaid.

### 2.4 Benefits During FMLA

Acme Corp will continue group health insurance coverage during FMLA leave under the same terms as if the employee had continued working. Employees must continue to pay their employee share of premiums. If an employee fails to return from FMLA, Acme Corp may recover the employer-paid premium costs.

## 3. Parental Leave

### 3.1 Entitlement

Acme Corp provides paid parental leave to all eligible employees upon the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. This leave is separate from and in addition to any FMLA entitlement.

**Eligibility:** Employees must have completed a minimum of **6 months of continuous employment** prior to the leave start date.

### 3.2 Leave Duration and Pay

| Role                  | Duration    | Pay Rate                  |
|-----------------------|-------------|---------------------------|
| Primary caregiver     | 16 weeks    | 100% of base salary       |
| Secondary caregiver   | 6 weeks     | 100% of base salary       |

Primary caregiver status is self-certified and is not restricted by gender or biological relationship. Employees may designate primary or secondary caregiver status at their discretion.

Both parents employed at Acme Corp may each take their full entitlement concurrently or at separate times, within 12 months of the qualifying event.

### 3.3 Timing and Flexibility

- Leave may begin up to **2 weeks before** the expected due date for birthing parents.
- Parental leave does not need to be taken in one continuous block; employees may take it in a minimum of **2-week increments** within 12 months of the qualifying event, subject to manager and HR approval.
- Unused parental leave does not carry over beyond 12 months and has no cash value.

### 3.4 Adoption and Foster Care

For adoptions and foster placements:
- Leave begins on the date of placement or finalization, whichever comes first.
- Reasonable paid time off (up to 3 days, charged to PTO) is also available for required pre-placement court appearances or agency visits.

## 4. Medical Leave

### 4.1 Non-FMLA Medical Leave

Employees who are not eligible for FMLA or who have exhausted FMLA entitlement may request a medical leave of absence for their own serious health condition. Acme Corp will consider such requests based on business needs, the employee's tenure, and the nature of the condition.

Non-FMLA medical leave may be approved for up to **8 additional weeks** beyond FMLA exhaustion at Acme Corp's discretion. This leave is unpaid except for remaining accrued PTO and applicable disability benefits.

### 4.2 Short-Term Disability Integration

Medical leave for an employee's own health condition automatically triggers review for Short-Term Disability (STD) benefits after a **7-calendar-day elimination period**. STD pays 60% of base salary for up to 12 weeks. See POL-BEN-005, Section 5.3.

### 4.3 Long-Term Disability Transition

If an employee remains unable to work after STD is exhausted (generally 12 weeks), Long-Term Disability (LTD) benefits activate after a 90-calendar-day elimination period. See POL-BEN-005, Section 5.4.

### 4.4 Medical Certification

Employees requesting medical leave must provide certification from a licensed healthcare provider on the standard Acme Corp Medical Certification Form (available in Workday). Certification must be returned within **15 calendar days** of the leave request. Acme Corp may request recertification every 30 days or when circumstances change.

## 5. Military Leave

### 5.1 Uniformed Services Leave (USERRA)

Acme Corp complies fully with the Uniformed Services Employment and Reemployment Rights Act (USERRA). Employees who need leave for any military service—including active duty, training, and reserve service—are entitled to job-protected leave for the duration of service.

### 5.2 Pay During Military Leave

- **Short-term military service (30 days or less):** Acme Corp pays the difference between the employee's military pay and their Acme Corp base salary for up to **30 calendar days** per calendar year.
- **Extended military deployment (31+ days):** Acme Corp pays the salary differential described above for the first **30 days** only. Remaining leave is unpaid except for accrued PTO the employee elects to use.

### 5.3 Benefits During Military Leave

Employees on military leave may elect to continue group health coverage for up to 24 months at the employee's expense (not COBRA rates). After 31 days of military leave, the employee's health insurance may be continued under the standard employer/employee cost-sharing arrangement for up to the first 24 months.

### 5.4 Reemployment Rights

Employees returning from qualifying military leave are entitled to reemployment in the position they would have held had they not been absent (the "escalator" principle), with full restoration of seniority, pay, and benefits, provided they return within the timeframes specified by USERRA.

## 6. Personal Leave of Absence

### 6.1 Eligibility and Approval

Employees with at least **1 year of continuous service** may request an unpaid personal leave of absence for personal reasons not covered by other leave types (e.g., family obligations, personal projects, travel). Personal LOA is a discretionary benefit, not an entitlement.

Approval requires:
- Written request submitted to People Operations at least **30 calendar days** in advance
- Manager approval
- HR and VP of People approval

### 6.2 Duration

Personal leave may be approved for a maximum of **8 weeks** within a rolling 12-month period.

### 6.3 Pay and Benefits

Personal leave is unpaid. Employees may use accrued PTO at the start of the leave period. Health insurance continues at the employee's full expense for the duration of the leave.

### 6.4 No Guarantee of Return

While Acme Corp makes reasonable efforts to hold the employee's position during personal leave, job protection is not guaranteed. If the business need changes, Acme Corp may fill the position. In such cases, Acme Corp will make good-faith efforts to place the returning employee in a comparable role.

## 7. Bereavement Leave

### 7.1 Entitlement

Bereavement leave is paid and does not draw from PTO. Entitlements are:

| Relationship                                         | Paid Leave |
|------------------------------------------------------|------------|
| Spouse, domestic partner, or child                   | 5 days     |
| Parent (biological, adoptive, step, or in-law)       | 3 days     |
| Sibling, grandparent, grandchild                     | 3 days     |
| Close friend or other family member (at HR discretion) | 1 day   |
| Pregnancy loss (employee or partner experiencing)    | 3 days     |

### 7.2 Additional Time

Employees needing additional time beyond the entitlement above may use accrued PTO. Unpaid bereavement time beyond PTO exhaustion may be approved by the manager with HR concurrence.

### 7.3 Documentation

Acme Corp does not require documentation for bereavement leave, but reserves the right to request verification (obituary, service program) if patterns suggest abuse of the policy.

## 8. Jury Duty and Witness Leave

### 8.1 Jury Duty

Employees summoned for jury duty will receive their full base salary for the duration of jury service, up to **4 weeks** per calendar year. Jury duty beyond 4 weeks is unpaid (except for remaining accrued PTO at the employee's election) unless required by applicable state law to be longer.

Employees must notify their manager as soon as the jury summons is received and provide a copy of the summons to People Operations via Workday.

### 8.2 Witness Leave

Employees required to appear in court as a witness (not as a party to the case) are entitled to unpaid time off for the court appearance, or may use accrued PTO. Employees subpoenaed as a witness in company-related legal matters will be paid their regular salary for time spent in court.

## 9. Domestic Violence, Sexual Assault, and Stalking Leave

Acme Corp provides job-protected leave to employees who are victims of domestic violence, sexual assault, or stalking for purposes of:
- Seeking medical attention for injuries
- Obtaining safety planning assistance
- Meeting with an attorney or victim advocate
- Attending legal proceedings

Leave duration and pay continuation are determined by applicable state law. California, New York, and other states with specific provisions will be addressed in the applicable state addendum. Employees requesting this leave should contact People Operations confidentially.

## 10. Leave Request and Administration

### 10.1 Requesting Leave

All leaves of absence must be initiated through People Operations via:
- **Workday:** Submit a Leave of Absence request for tracked LOA types
- **Email:** people-ops@acmecorp.com for sensitive or complex situations

Employees should provide as much advance notice as possible. For foreseeable leaves (planned surgery, birth), at least **30 days' notice** is expected. For unforeseeable leaves, notice must be given as soon as practicable.

### 10.2 Continuous vs. Intermittent Leave

Leave may be taken as:
- **Continuous:** Full days off for an extended period
- **Intermittent:** Partial or full days taken as needed (e.g., for recurring medical appointments or chronic conditions)
- **Reduced schedule:** A temporarily reduced workday or work week

Intermittent leave must be scheduled as far in advance as medical necessity allows and must minimize disruption to business operations.

### 10.3 Communication During Leave

Employees on leave are generally not expected to perform work duties. However, they may be contacted by People Operations for administrative purposes (benefit elections, documentation). Any work communication must be approved by HR and kept to the absolute minimum necessary.

## 11. Return to Work

### 11.1 Return Notification

Employees must notify People Operations and their manager **at least 5 business days** before their expected return date so that workspace, access, and team logistics can be prepared.

### 11.2 Fitness for Duty

Employees returning from medical leave may be required to provide a healthcare provider's note confirming they are cleared to return to full duty (or specifying any work restrictions). Acme Corp will make reasonable accommodations for documented restrictions.

### 11.3 Reinstatement

Employees returning from FMLA, parental, military, or other legally protected leave are entitled to reinstatement to the same or an equivalent position (same pay, benefits, and terms of employment). Return from personal leave does not carry the same job-protection guarantee (see Section 6.4).
