## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy sets the standards for professional conduct, ethical behavior, and respectful workplace interactions at Acme Corp. These standards apply to all employees, contractors, interns, and vendors—whether working on-site, remotely, or at any company-sponsored event—and to all interactions conducted on company platforms, email, and Slack.

Acme Corp is committed to maintaining a workplace where every person is treated with dignity and respect. Violations of this policy are taken seriously and may result in disciplinary action up to and including immediate termination.

---

## 2. Professional Standards

### 2.1 Core Behavioral Expectations

All Acme Corp employees are expected to:
- Act with honesty, integrity, and transparency in all business dealings.
- Treat colleagues, customers, vendors, and members of the public with professionalism and respect.
- Perform their duties to the best of their ability and meet reasonable work standards.
- Follow the directions of their manager and adhere to all company policies.
- Represent Acme Corp professionally in all external interactions, including email, social media, and in-person meetings.

### 2.2 Collaboration and Communication

Acme Corp values a culture of direct, respectful communication:
- Critique ideas, not individuals.
- Provide feedback in a constructive and specific manner.
- Escalate disagreements through appropriate channels rather than allowing them to fester or become personal.
- Be responsive and timely in communications during core business hours.

### 2.3 Confidentiality

Employees must protect confidential business information, including but not limited to financial data, product roadmaps, customer data, personnel information, and trade secrets. This obligation continues after separation. See the Data Security Policy (POL-SEC-004) for detailed data handling requirements.

## 3. Anti-Harassment and Anti-Discrimination Policy

### 3.1 Commitment Statement

Acme Corp prohibits harassment and discrimination of any kind based on:
- Race, color, ethnicity, or national origin
- Sex, gender identity, gender expression, or sexual orientation
- Age (40 and over), disability (physical or mental), or pregnancy
- Religion or creed
- Veteran or military status
- Genetic information
- Any other characteristic protected by federal, state, or local law

This prohibition applies to all employment decisions, including hiring, compensation, promotion, assignments, and termination.

### 3.2 What Constitutes Harassment

Harassment includes any **unwelcome conduct** based on a protected characteristic that:
- Creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive work environment, **or**
- Results in an adverse employment action (quid pro quo harassment)

**Examples of prohibited conduct** (not exhaustive):
- Offensive jokes, slurs, or derogatory comments
- Unwanted physical contact
- Displaying offensive images, memes, or symbols
- Persistent unwanted flirting, romantic overtures, or sexual comments
- Threats, intimidation, or bullying
- Deliberate misuse of a person's name or pronouns after correction
- Exclusion from meetings, projects, or activities based on protected characteristics

### 3.3 Sexual Harassment

Sexual harassment is a specific form of prohibited harassment and includes:
- **Quid pro quo:** Submission to sexual conduct as a condition of employment or employment benefits
- **Hostile environment:** Conduct of a sexual nature that unreasonably interferes with an employee's work performance or creates a hostile work environment

Both in-person and virtual sexual harassment (e.g., via Slack, email, Zoom) are covered.

### 3.4 Scope

This policy applies to conduct by any employee against any other person, including managers, direct reports, peers, contractors, customers, and vendors, in any work-related context.

## 4. Reporting Harassment or Discrimination

### 4.1 How to Report

Employees who experience or witness harassment, discrimination, or any violation of this policy are encouraged to report it promptly. Reports may be made to:
- **Direct manager** (if not the subject of the complaint)
- **HR Business Partner or People Operations:** people-ops@acmecorp.com
- **Anonymous Ethics Hotline:** ethics-hotline.acmecorp.com (24/7, anonymous option available)
- **Legal Department:** legal@acmecorp.com (for complex legal matters)

### 4.2 Retaliation Prohibition

**Retaliation against any employee who reports a concern in good faith, participates in an investigation, or opposes conduct they believe to be unlawful is strictly prohibited.** Retaliation is itself a serious violation of this policy and will be treated with the same severity as the underlying complaint.

Retaliation includes adverse employment actions (demotion, termination, unfavorable assignments), social exclusion, hostile treatment, and any other conduct that would discourage a reasonable person from reporting.

### 4.3 Investigation Process

All reports are taken seriously and investigated promptly and thoroughly:
1. People Operations acknowledges receipt within **2 business days**.
2. An investigation is initiated, typically led by an HR Business Partner or outside counsel for senior-level complaints.
3. Both the complainant and the respondent have the opportunity to provide their account and evidence.
4. Investigations are conducted confidentially to the extent practicable.
5. Findings and any disciplinary action are communicated to both parties to the extent appropriate.

Investigations are completed within **30 calendar days** of the initial report, with extensions possible for complex matters.

## 5. Conflict of Interest

### 5.1 Definition

A conflict of interest arises when an employee's personal interests—financial, relational, or otherwise—could influence or appear to influence their business decisions or loyalty to Acme Corp.

### 5.2 Common Conflict Situations

Examples of potential conflicts that must be disclosed:
- Ownership (more than 1% equity) in a vendor, customer, or competitor
- Employment or consulting work for a vendor, customer, or competitor
- Hiring, supervising, or evaluating a family member or romantic partner
- Receiving personal gifts or benefits from vendors or customers above the threshold in Section 7.2
- Serving on the board of a competitor or directly competing company

### 5.3 Disclosure Requirement

Employees must disclose actual or potential conflicts of interest to their manager and People Operations **as soon as they arise**. Disclosure does not automatically mean the relationship is prohibited; many conflicts can be managed through recusal, oversight changes, or other mitigating measures. **Failure to disclose is the violation**, not merely having the conflict.

Annual conflict of interest disclosures are required of all employees as part of the performance review cycle.

## 6. Social Media Policy

### 6.1 Personal Social Media Use

Employees are free to use personal social media platforms, subject to the following:
- Do not disclose Confidential or Restricted company information (see POL-SEC-004, Section 2.1).
- Do not speak on behalf of Acme Corp without explicit authorization from Marketing or Communications.
- Do not post content that constitutes harassment, discrimination, or threats toward colleagues, customers, or competitors.
- Include a disclaimer ("views are my own") when discussing topics relevant to Acme Corp's business.

### 6.2 Company Social Media Accounts

Only employees specifically authorized by the Marketing team may post to official Acme Corp social media accounts (LinkedIn, X, Instagram, etc.). Unauthorized posts to official accounts are a policy violation.

### 6.3 Public Commentary on the Company

Employees who make public commentary (blog posts, conference talks, media interviews) about Acme Corp's products, culture, or business strategy must obtain clearance from the Communications team **before publishing**.

## 7. Gifts, Entertainment, and Anti-Corruption

### 7.1 Receiving Gifts

Employees may accept gifts from vendors, customers, or business partners only if:
- The gift is of modest value (**$75 or less** per year per source)
- It would not create an obligation or expectation of preferential treatment
- It is not cash or a cash equivalent (gift cards, vouchers)

Gifts exceeding $75 must be declined or, if physically received (e.g., a food basket), shared with the team and disclosed to the manager. Entertainment (meals, events) is subject to the limits in the Expense Reimbursement Policy (POL-EXP-001, Section 4.4).

### 7.2 Giving Gifts

Gifts given to customers or vendors on behalf of Acme Corp must be approved in advance by the employee's manager and comply with the Expense Reimbursement Policy. Gifts of cash or cash equivalents to external parties are prohibited.

### 7.3 Anti-Bribery

Acme Corp strictly prohibits bribery in any form—offering, giving, receiving, or soliciting anything of value to improperly influence a business decision or government action. This prohibition applies globally. Violations may expose both the individual and Acme Corp to significant criminal and civil liability.

## 8. Drugs and Alcohol

### 8.1 Workplace Prohibition

Employees must not:
- Report to work or perform job duties while impaired by alcohol, illegal drugs, or misused prescription medications
- Use, possess, distribute, or sell alcohol or illegal substances on company premises or at company events (except at approved company-sponsored events with sanctioned alcohol service)

### 8.2 Company-Sponsored Events

Alcohol may be available at company-sponsored events (offsites, team celebrations). Employees are expected to drink responsibly. Acme Corp does not cover expenses for excessive alcohol consumption and reserves the right to address conduct at events under this policy.

### 8.3 Prescription Medications

Employees taking prescription medications that may affect performance, alertness, or motor skills should notify their manager of any potential work restrictions (without disclosing the specific medical condition) and cooperate on necessary accommodations.

## 9. Workplace Safety and Violence Prevention

### 9.1 Safe Work Environment

Acme Corp is committed to providing a safe workplace free from violence, intimidation, and threatening behavior. All employees have a responsibility to maintain this standard.

### 9.2 Prohibited Conduct

The following are strictly prohibited:
- Physical violence, assault, or battery
- Verbal or written threats of violence against any person
- Possessing weapons on company premises (including firearms, even with a license, unless you are authorized law enforcement)
- Intimidation, coercion, or threatening behavior

### 9.3 Reporting Threats

Any employee who receives or witnesses a threat of violence must report it **immediately** to their manager, HR, or security, or call 911 if the threat is imminent. Acme Corp will investigate all threats and take appropriate protective action.

## 10. Dress Code

### 10.1 In-Office Standards

Acme Corp maintains a **business casual** dress code in all office locations. Attire should be neat, professional, and appropriate for a workplace setting. Clothing with offensive graphics, language, or imagery is prohibited.

### 10.2 Client Meetings and Formal Events

Business formal or smart business attire is expected for client presentations, executive meetings, and formal company events unless otherwise communicated by the event organizer.

### 10.3 Remote Work

While working remotely, employees attending video calls are expected to dress in at minimum business casual attire from the waist up for any call where video is enabled, particularly for client or executive-facing meetings.

## 11. Disciplinary Process

### 11.1 Principles

Acme Corp follows a fair and consistent disciplinary process. Disciplinary outcomes are commensurate with the severity, frequency, and context of the misconduct.

### 11.2 Progressive Discipline

For most conduct violations, Acme Corp follows a progressive discipline model:
1. **Verbal counseling** — informal discussion with manager documenting concern
2. **Written warning** — formal written documentation placed in personnel file
3. **Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)** — structured improvement plan with timeline
4. **Final written warning or suspension**
5. **Termination**

Not every step must be followed in every case. Severe violations (harassment, violence, fraud, theft, breach of data security, or illegal activity) may result in **immediate termination** at any stage.

### 11.3 Investigation Prior to Termination

Before terminating an employee for cause, Acme Corp will conduct a reasonable investigation to gather facts. The employee may be placed on **paid administrative leave** during the investigation period.
